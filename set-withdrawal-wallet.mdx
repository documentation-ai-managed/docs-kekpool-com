# No-KYC user.

If you start mining as a **no-KYC user** (without registering), your **wallet address** is taken from the username configured on your ASIC miner.

For example, if your miner is configured as:

`kaspa:q3oyic2ce4ok9e3vhjj3cm48emzxr1plmpfrtq5lmir9tq4ef1e7ptrbmka0j.worker1`

we will use:

`kaspa:q3oyic2ce4ok9e3vhjj3cm48emzxr1plmpfrtq5lmir9tq4ef1e7ptrbmka0j`

as your withdrawal wallet. This wallet address **cannot be changed** on kekpool.com.

For any **merge-mined coins**, you must specify your withdrawal wallet in the miner's **password** field (which is normally set to `x`).

For example, to receive ZKS payouts, set the password field to:

`zkas=zkas:434fjdnfkasfkdsfdjffdf`

In this case, we will use:

`zkas:434fjdnfkasfkdsfdjffdf`

as your withdrawal wallet for ZKS payouts.
