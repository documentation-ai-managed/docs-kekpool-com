# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2026

Welcome to **KekPool** (“we”, “our”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the KekPool website and mining pool services available at **https://kekpool.com**.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

- Email address  
- Account username  
- Wallet address  
- Support messages or communications  

We do not require identity documents or personal identification unless explicitly stated.

---

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use KekPool, we may automatically collect:

- IP address  
- Browser type and version  
- Operating system  
- Device information  
- Access time and usage statistics  

This data is used strictly for system operation, security, and analytics.

---

### 1.3 Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

- Maintain login sessions  
- Remember preferences  
- Improve website performance  

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Operate and maintain the mining pool  
- Display mining statistics and rewards  
- Secure accounts and prevent abuse  
- Communicate important service notices  
- Improve system reliability and performance  

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information

We may share information only in the following cases:

- When required by law or legal request

We never share personal information for marketing purposes.

Your information is stored only for as long as necessary to:
