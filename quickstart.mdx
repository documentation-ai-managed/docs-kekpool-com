## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have the following ready:

<Callout kind="info">

- A Kaspa wallet to receive payouts (e.g., create one via the official Kaspa wallet)
- Mining hardware: GPU with at least 6GB VRAM or Kaspa ASIC miner
- Stable internet connection with low latency `<50ms` to the pool server
- Operating system: Windows, Linux, or macOS

</Callout>

KEKPOOL supports Kaspa (`KAS`) mining via the kHeavyHash algorithm. Payouts occur daily with fees as low as `0.9%`. Your hashrate contributes to the pool's total, and you earn proportional rewards.

## Create Your KEKPOOL Account

Sign up to access the dashboard for real-time monitoring of your hashrate, earnings, and active workers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Registration Page" icon="user-plus">
    Go to [https://kekpool.com/register](https://kekpool.com/register).
    
    Fill in your email, username, and password. Agree to terms.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification email from KEKPOOL.
    
    Click the link to activate your account. This enables payouts and dashboard access.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Log In and Get Wallet Address" icon="log-in">
    Log in at [https://kekpool.com/login](https://kekpool.com/login).
    
    Navigate to the Wallet tab and add your Kaspa payout address (format: `kaspa:qrq...`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download and Install Mining Software

KEKPOOL recommends GPU miners like lolMiner or BzMiner for Kaspa. Download the latest release from their official GitHub repositories.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="lolMiner (Linux/Windows)" icon="download">
    Download from [GitHub](https://github.com/Lolliedieb/lolMiner-releases/releases).
    
    Extract the archive and make it executable on Linux:
    
    ````bash
    chmod +x lolMiner_v1.XX_linux.tar.gz
    tar -xzf lolMiner_v1.XX_linux.tar.gz
    cd 1.0/lolMiner
    ````
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="BzMiner (Multi-OS)" icon="package">
    Download from [GitHub](https://github.com/doktor83/bzMiner/releases).
    
    Extract and prepare:
    
    ````bash
    unzip bzMiner-windows-portable.zip
    cd bzMiner
    ````
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Connect Your Miner to KEKPOOL

Configure your miner to point to the KEKPOOL stratum server. Use your KEKPOOL username as the worker name (e.g., `yourusername.rig1`).

Replace `YOUR_WALLET` with your Kaspa address and `YOUR_USERNAME` with your pool username.

<CodeGroup tabs="lolMiner,BzMiner" show-lines="true">
  ````bash
  ./lolMiner --algo KASPA --pool stratum+tcp://kekpool.com:8443 --user YOUR_USERNAME.YOUR_RIG --pass x
  ````
  
  ````bash
  bzminer -a kheavyhash -o stratum+tcp://kekpool.com:8443 -u YOUR_USERNAME.YOUR_RIG -p x
  ````
</CodeGroup>

<Callout kind="tip">

Monitor the console output for `Connected to kekpool.com` and your hashrate (e.g., `15.2 GH/s`). Allow `5-10` minutes for shares to register on the dashboard.

</Callout>

## Verify Connection and Monitor

1. Log in to [https://dashboard.kekpool.com](https://dashboard.kekpool.com).
2. Check Workers tab: Your rig should show `Online` status and hashrate.
3. View Pool stats: Track global hashrate and your share.

If no shares appear, verify firewall settings and stratum URL.
