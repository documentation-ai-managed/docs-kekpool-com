---
title: Miner Not Showing Up
description: What to do if your miner doesn't appear on kekpool.com.
---

## Symptoms

- Miner is running but not listed on the pool dashboard
- No reported hashrate or shares

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check settings">
    Confirm your mining address, pool URL, and port match those on kekpool.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Firewall & network">
    Ensure outbound ports for mining are open and not blocked by a firewall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart miner">
    Stop and restart your mining software after verifying settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait 2–3 minutes">
    It may take up to 3 minutes for new miners to appear in the pool stats.
  </Step>
</Steps>
