---
title: Start Mining Kaspa with KEKPOOL
description: Begin mining Kaspa (KAS) on kekpool.com in minutes.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a Kaspa wallet">
    Download an official Kaspa wallet and copy your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download lolMiner">
    Get the latest release from the official GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your start command">
    ````bash
    lolMiner -a KASPA -p stratum+tcp://kas.kekpool.com:port -u YOUR_KASPA_ADDRESS
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start mining">
    Run the command. Check your stats at `https://kekpool.com` using your wallet address.
  </Step>
</Steps>
